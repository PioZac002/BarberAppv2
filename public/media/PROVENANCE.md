# Media provenance

`cut-before.jpg` / `cut-after.jpg` (+ `@0.5` variants)
: The same sitter, chair, lighting and framing before and after a cut and beard
  trim. Supplied by the project owner for this build as
  `before_barber.png` / `after_barber.png`; resized and re-encoded here, not
  otherwise retouched. They are the only photographic rasters in the product.

`room-floor.jpg`, `room-work.jpg`, `room-pole.jpg`, `room-tools.jpg`,
`room-towels.jpg` (+ `@sm` variants where the source allowed one)
: The project owner's supplied shop photographs `barbers1`–`barbers5`:
  the floor under its hexagonal lighting, barbers at work, the pole by the
  door, the tool drawer and the towel shelves. Resized and re-encoded only.
  `room-towels` (401px) and `room-tools` (524px) are small originals and are
  never asked to fill a large frame; nothing here is upscaled.

`back-bar.jpg`, `back-bar@0.66.jpg`
: The project owner's supplied `products_view.jpg`, the shop's product wall.
  Resized and re-encoded only.

The chair orbit that previously ran here was removed at the owner's request;
its master stays in `design-assets/barber_chair_rotating.gif` and no part of
it ships.

`../brand/mark.png`, `../brand/lockup.png`
: Derived from the project owner's supplied `logo_barber.png`. The soft blue
  halo around the artwork was removed by rebuilding the alpha channel from
  per-pixel whiteness, leaving the white artwork on true transparency so it can
  be inked from CSS in either theme. No other alteration.

`../icons/icon-*.png`, `../favicon.png`
: The mark above, centred on the glaze (#1D2FC9). See ../icons/PROVENANCE.md.
